FROM THE OFFICE TO THE LAST LAWN

One workday. Everyone on the same page.

The owner sets up the work in Office. The crew carries the details into the field. Each confirmed update helps the business see what is happening without chasing a text thread.

  1. 1

    Set the day in Office

    Add the customer, schedule the visit, list the services and assign a verified crew member. Include gate instructions and any details the crew needs before arriving.

  2. 2

    Open today’s jobs

    Sign into Crew with the email your owner assigned. See your stops, completed work and the next job. Open a stop to review its address, services and notes.

  3. 3

    Navigate and check in

    Open directions for the next stop. At the property, confirm check-in or Start job. If location is unavailable, use a manual check-in reason.

  4. 4

    Work through the details

    Follow the service list and access notes. If a gate is locked or something needs attention, report the issue so Office can see the blocker.

  5. 5

    Save proof. Confirm completion.

    Add a completion photo, check off the services and confirm Job Complete. Jobs that require proof must have a saved photo before they can be completed.

  6. 6

    Sync and move to the next stop

    Confirmed updates return to Office. If connectivity drops, supported job updates and photos remain on the device pending sync. Keep the app open to finish syncing when you reconnect.
