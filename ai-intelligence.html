THE SIGNALS BEHIND A SMARTER DAY

More context. Less guesswork.

01 / WEATHER

Plan for the conditions.

Use the service location to look at local forecasts and weather risks. Review rain interruptions, heat and seasonal changes when planning the week.

Current weather guidance uses the National Weather Service. Recommendations do not automatically move confirmed visits.

02 / LOCATION

Know where the work happens.

Connect the property address with a crew’s job-start or completion location, when browser permission is granted. Use nearby jobs and crew capacity to review a tighter plan.

Crew uses a mobile website. Location can pause when a phone locks or the browser goes into the background; manual job updates remain important.

03 / PHOTO RECORDS

See before. See after.

Keep original photos attached to the job. Optional analysis can surface visible changes, areas needing attention and possible follow-up work for your review.

A photo shows the visible scene, not everything about a property. Matching camera angles make comparisons more useful.

04 / YARD INSIGHTS

Understand what’s growing.

Ask for likely grass, plant, tree and shrub identifications from clear photos. Review possible care needs and ask for closer pictures when identification is uncertain.

AI suggestions are not confirmed species identification, disease diagnosis or pesticide instructions. A qualified professional should verify treatment needs.

05 / SCHEDULING

Make the week fit the work.

Bring weather guidance, service frequency, location and estimated labor into planning. Review seasonal care intervals and route changes before agreeing to a new schedule.

Growth rates vary by grass type, irrigation and local conditions. Seasonal scheduling rules and automated changes require configuration and validation.

06 / CUSTOMER OPTIONS

Offer the right next service.

Turn a reviewed observation into a priced option: shrub trimming, edging, mulch refresh or another service your company provides. Explain the need with the original job photos.

The operator chooses the scope and price. Homeowners explicitly approve added work; an AI suggestion never creates an automatic charge.

HOW IT FITS YOUR DAY

Capture. Review. Plan. Approve.

  1. Start with the property.

    Confirm the address, recurring services, access instructions and customer preferences.

  2. Capture the visit.

    The crew confirms job start and completion, and adds clear before-and-after photos.

  3. Review the insight.

    The owner checks optional AI observations against the photos, local conditions and their knowledge of the yard.

  4. Build the next step.

    Use your configured rates and labor estimates to prepare a care option or review a schedule recommendation.

  5. Let the customer choose.

    Share the scope, photos, price and timing. Add the work only after approval.
