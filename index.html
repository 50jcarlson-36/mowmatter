A few things worth knowing.

Is the assessment a formal business valuation?

No. It summarizes your inputs. A supported valuation needs reliable profit history, retention, transferability and relevant market evidence. This page does not assign an unsupported sale price.

Are routing and payment services connected yet?

Not yet. This is the first release: business assessment and growth planning. Live operational services will be added through their required integrations.

Does the calculator guarantee savings or new customers?

No. It shows scenarios based on your choices and the stated assumptions. Actual performance depends on your jobs, costs, market and execution.

Can I keep my own company name?

That is the plan. Mow Matter is designed to support independent businesses and their existing brands.
