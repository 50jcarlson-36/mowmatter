Manage a subscription

Use the enabled Office billing portal to view invoices and manage cancellation. A monthly software subscription renews until cancelled according to its accepted checkout terms. The current portal is configured for cancellation at the end of the paid period; billing requests are reviewed according to the accepted offer and applicable law. Do not assume a refund is forbidden where law or the accepted offer requires one.

Homeowner payments

Homeowners pay their lawn operator. Service refunds and disputes concern that operator’s accepted service agreement. Payout availability depends on Stripe account status. Software subscriptions do not authorize lawn-service autopay.

Optional add-ons

Instant Quote add-on price is not finalized. A lookup trial does not authorize automatic billing. Any paid offer must display price, usage, renewal and cancellation information before acceptance.

Questions

Use the contact/request form for platform billing questions. Use the operator contact on your booking for lawn-service concerns.
