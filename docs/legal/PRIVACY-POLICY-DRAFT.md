## 2. Information we collect
### Lawn-care owners
We collect account/contact information, business details, team membership and permissions, customer contact lists, service addresses, schedules, routes, service plans, prices, invoice/payment references, manual accounting entries, inquiries and communications. Operators must have authority to provide these records and should avoid unnecessary sensitive information. Your uploaded customer database remains your business information, subject to the rights of the individuals it describes.

### Field crews: location and work records
We collect crew account and assignment information, job instructions, check-in/start/completion times, submitted location coordinates and accuracy, work notes, issue reports and completion photos. Photos can reveal property details or location. Do not intentionally include unrelated people, private interiors or sensitive documents.

**Current Crew web app:** location is captured in the foreground when supported and permitted, to help identify a nearby job and prompt a worker to confirm start or completion. Location does not itself confirm that work occurred. Permission denial, weak GPS and unsupported devices can require manual confirmation. A web app is not a guarantee of continuous or background tracking.

**If continuous or background tracking is introduced:** before enabling it, we will display a separate, prominent notice explaining the feature, data collected, purpose, who can see it, retention and controls. The app will request the necessary device permissions. Tracking must be limited to disclosed work periods, visibly indicate when active and provide stop/pause controls. Operators must satisfy applicable worker-notice, employment and privacy requirements; permission to use a phone is not unlimited consent to employee surveillance. We do not represent background tracking as active in the current release. Device permissions can be changed in device/browser settings, and disabling location may limit automatic routing features.

### Homeowners
We collect names, email/phone contact details, physical service addresses, requested services, preferred frequency/dates, property/access notes, quotes, booking/service history, communications and notification preferences. Avoid submitting gate codes or other security-sensitive instructions through public inquiries. A provider introduction or booking occurs only through the disclosed confirmation process.

Stripe collects payment-card and bank information through its payment components or hosted pages. Mow Matter receives payment references, status, amounts and limited payment-method details as supplied by Stripe; we do not intentionally store full card numbers or security codes. Connected-account verification information may be collected directly by Stripe under its privacy terms.

### Visitors, devices and preferences
We may process browser/device information, operational logs, campaign source labels and basic site events. Current site preferences may be stored locally, such as banner dismissal or measurement choice. Optional measurement should run only after the applicable preference is selected. Third-party infrastructure can maintain security logs. A cookie/storage notice explains the configured tools; a preference banner is not proof that every tracking tool is disabled. We honor applicable legally required privacy signals when the relevant processing is enabled.

## 3. How we use information
We use information to authenticate accounts, apply company/role permissions, manage customers and crews, schedule work, display assigned jobs, support permitted navigation/routing, generate and review quotes, verify services through job events/photos, provide approved service communications, process authorized payments, administer subscriptions, investigate complaints, support users and secure the platform.

Planning, equity and route-efficiency tools may use operational records to produce estimates and scenarios. They are not lender appraisals, guaranteed sale prices or promises of profits. AI/providers receive only information necessary for an enabled feature; identify material new providers before activation. Optional marketing is separate from service notices. We do not use an owner’s identifiable customer database to advertise competing operators to those customers without a lawful basis and appropriate disclosure.

## 4. Sharing and third parties
### Sharing needed to fulfill lawn services
An owner and authorized assigned crew may receive relevant homeowner contact, property, scope, access and schedule details. Homeowners may receive their own quote, visit status, approved proof photos, invoice information and provider contact. Crew access should be limited to operational records appropriate to their role. Owners may view their business’s crew activity, including location records collected for disclosed work purposes. We do not expose a tenant’s customer book or routes to unrelated operators.

We use Supabase for authentication, operational databases, functions and private photo storage; Render for application hosting; Resend for configured email; Stripe for payments/account verification; Cloudflare for configured DNS/security functions; and RealEstateAPI for property information. Each may process data necessary for its role. Confirm the final provider list and processing regions before publication.

Google Maps or another mapping provider may receive addresses, coordinates and route requests when its navigation/mapping features are used. Opening an external navigation link is also subject to that provider’s terms and privacy policy. External Google Maps navigation is distinct from an integrated traffic-aware optimizer; do not imply an optimization API is currently connected when it is not. Google sign-in, if enabled, provides identity information such as name/email according to the scopes granted, not access to unrelated Google account content by default.

Provider links: https://stripe.com/privacy ; https://policies.google.com/privacy ; https://supabase.com/privacy ; https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy ; https://render.com/privacy

We may disclose information when legally required, to protect lawful rights and safety, or in a properly disclosed business transaction with appropriate safeguards. A sale of an operator’s business does not automatically transfer customer permissions, payment authorizations or account credentials. We do not sell identifiable customer databases as part of the ordinary platform service. Review applicable state definitions of sale/sharing and any future advertising integrations before making broader legal claims.

## 5. Security and retention
We use safeguards appropriate to the service, including company/role authorization, restricted server credentials, private photo storage, time-limited access links and secure transport. Uploaded databases should not be publicly readable. No system is absolutely secure, and users must protect credentials and promptly remove departed crew access.

We retain information for the purposes described, account/service administration, security, dispute resolution and applicable legal obligations. Retention depends on category and need, rather than a blanket indefinite period. **Before final publication, adopt a specific schedule for leads, job photos, location data, inactive accounts, financial/audit records and backups.** Location and photos should not be retained longer than needed for disclosed operational purposes. Account closure does not necessarily erase required payment or legal records; retained records should have restricted access. Backup deletion may follow a documented aging cycle.

## 6. Choices and privacy rights
Depending on location and law, individuals may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of information, restrict certain processing, withdraw consent, and opt out of covered sale/sharing, targeted advertising or profiling. Some jurisdictions provide appeal rights. We do not require marketing consent to request service or use an ordinary paid account.

Submit a request through the contact/privacy-request page or [Insert Monitored Legal Email]. We verify identity and authority proportionately before disclosing or deleting records and do not reveal whether another person has an account. Some operator-controlled information may require coordination with the operator. We will respond within applicable deadlines, explain lawful exceptions and provide appeal instructions where required. No adverse treatment for exercising protected rights.

Adjust device permissions to control location. Use marketing preferences to stop promotional messages; essential account/service notices may continue where permitted. Information voluntarily submitted in support should be limited to what is needed. Do not submit payment-card numbers, passwords or identity documents through the public form.

## 7. Children, international use and changes
Account holders must be adults or otherwise permitted under applicable law and the operator’s reviewed worker policies. The platform is not directed to children under 13; contact us if their information was supplied. Do not assume all crew employment situations are covered by an age checkbox.

Providers may process information in countries different from your residence. Confirm and disclose applicable transfer mechanisms and regional requirements before launching outside the intended markets. We update this policy when practices change, identify the revision date and provide additional notice or consent where required. This draft does not certify compliance with every jurisdiction.
