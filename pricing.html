Know what’s included—and what’s extra.

Mow Matter platform fees on collected customer payments: Solo Starter 3.5% · Growth Route 2.5% · Scale & Crew 1.5%, plus Stripe processing fees. On a $100 customer payment, the platform fee is $3.50, $2.50 or $1.50 respectively. Homeowners pay their lawn company; the operator handles service refunds and disputes. Customer payment processing is enabled for operators who complete Stripe verification and bank setup in Office.

Credits apply to eligible AI tasks such as route re-optimization. Instant Quotes has its own add-on allowance. Credit costs per action, add-on packs, expiration and communication allowances will be published before billing launches. Choose your subscription in Office through secure Stripe checkout.
